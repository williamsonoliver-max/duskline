# Duskline Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

Duskline is built to work entirely on your device. This policy is short because there isn't much to say: **Duskline does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data with us or anyone else.** There is no backend server, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no advertising network built into the app.

## What Duskline stores, and where

Everything you enter — sleep sessions, shifts, and app settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it (see "Sharing," below). If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

## Calendar access

If you use "Import from Calendar" on the Schedule tab, Duskline asks iOS for permission to read your calendars. This is used only to read event start/end times and titles so you can bring them in as shifts — Duskline does not read, store, or transmit any other calendar data, and the import only touches the specific calendar you select.

## Notifications

Duskline can schedule local notifications (wind-down and wake reminders) for your recommended sleep window. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device through Apple's notification system — no push notification server is involved, and Duskline has no way to send you a notification remotely.

## Sharing (Family Sync)

When you use the share button to send your schedule to someone, Duskline generates an image on your device and hands it to iOS's native share sheet. Where that image goes from there — Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or anywhere else — is entirely up to you and happens through Apple's share sheet, not through Duskline.

## Third parties

Duskline does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is sold or shared with third parties, because no data leaves the app in the first place.

## Changes to this policy

If Duskline's data practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated to reflect that, and the update will be reflected in the app's release notes.
